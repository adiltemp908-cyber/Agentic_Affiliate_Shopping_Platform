A LITTLE LESS BLACK BOX

The reasoning is part
of the result.

Shortlist owns its catalog search and ranking. Your request is interpreted locally; retailer results are never bought or promoted into a higher position.

01

Understand the brief

Your assistant remembers your brief, understands follow-ups, and explains what the actual catalog can and cannot establish. It runs locally on your computer.

02

Apply real constraints

We check category, price, GPU, memory, clothing attributes and other recorded requirements. A missing fact cannot pass a must-have requirement. We don’t quietly relax your budget.

03

Search in two ways

BM25 finds literal product terms. Local sentence embeddings find related wording. Reciprocal-rank fusion combines both rankings after filtering. You can compare each baseline.

04

Keep evidence attached

Offers keep their source and observation time. Cross-store matching requires exact identifiers and variant fields. The assistant explains recorded differences, with unknowns clearly shown.

First release: a research tool.

Electronics, fashion, home and more, India / INR. Stock and shipping can change. Historical observations are opt-in. Review synthesis, delivery promises, and verified battery-life comparisons are not implemented. Purchase links are ordinary retailer links; affiliate tracking is disabled.
